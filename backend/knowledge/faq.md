# Sri Ramakrishna College of Arts & Science for Women (SRCW) - Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

## About SRCW Overview and Accreditation
Sri Ramakrishna College of Arts & Science for Women (SRCW), Coimbatore was established in 1991 by SNR Sons Charitable Trust. The institution is affiliated with Bharathiar University and is an Autonomous institution reaccredited by NAAC with an 'A+' Grade. SRCW is an ISO certified premier women's institution dedicated to women empowerment through holistic education, state-of-the-art infrastructure, value-based learning, and academic excellence. Tagline: "Educate to Empower".

## Programmes Offered - Undergraduate (UG)
SRCW offers diverse undergraduate degree programmes affiliated with Bharathiar University:

10. **BBA (Bachelor of Business Administration)**: 3 years. Management principles, HR, marketing, operations.
11. **BBA (Business Process Management - BPM)**: 3 years in association with TCS.
12. **B.Sc. Biochemistry**: 3 years. Molecular biology, clinical biochemistry, enzymology.
13. **B.Sc. Microbiology**: 3 years. Medical microbiology, immunology, industrial fermentation.
14. **B.Sc. Mathematics**: 3 years. Pure mathematics, applied statistics, operations research.
15. **B.A. English Literature**: 3 years. World literature, linguistics, journalism, creative writing.

## Admission Eligibility and How to Apply
- **Eligibility for UG**: Candidates must have passed Higher Secondary Examinations (10+2 / HSC) from Tamil Nadu State Board or CBSE / ICSE or equivalent recognized board with required core subjects.
  - Science/Computing streams: Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics / Computer Science / Biology.
  - Commerce streams: Commerce, Accountancy, Economics, Business Mathematics / Computer Applications.
- **Eligibility for PG**: A recognized Bachelor's degree in relevant discipline with minimum passing percentage specified by Bharathiar University guidelines.
- **Application Procedure**: Applications can be submitted online through the official admission portal at [https://srcw.ac.in/admissionenquiry](https://srcw.ac.in/admissionenquiry) or in person at the College Admissions Office.
- **Documents Required at Admission**:
  1. 10th and 12th Standard Original Mark Sheets and photocopies.
  2. Transfer Certificate (TC) and Conduct Certificate from the previous institution.
  3. Community Certificate (if applicable).
  4. Migration Certificate (for students from outside Tamil Nadu / other universities).
  5. Aadhaar Card photocopy.
  6. Recent passport size photographs.

## Scholarships and Financial Support
SRCW assists students in availing several government and institutional scholarship schemes:
1. **Management Merit Scholarships**: Concessions awarded to meritorious students scoring high marks in +2 examinations.
2. **Sports Scholarships**: Concessions in tuition fees and hostel fees for outstanding sportswomen representing District, State, and National tournaments.
3. **Government Scholarships**:

   - BC/MBC/DNC welfare scholarships.
   - Moovalur Ramamirtham Ammaiyar Higher Education Assurance Scheme (Pudhumaipen Scheme - Rs. 1000/month for government school girl students).

   - Central Sector Scheme of Scholarship for College and University Students.
4. **SNR Sons Charitable Trust Free Education Scheme**: Full fee waiver for economically underprivileged deserving students.

## Hostel Accommodation and Rules
SRCW provides safe, secure, and comfortable hostel accommodation exclusively for women on campus:
- **Facilities**: Well-ventilated rooms (2-sharing, 3-sharing, 4-sharing), individual cot, study table, chair, and wardrobe.
- **Mess**: Hygienic vegetarian and non-vegetarian food cooked in a modern steam kitchen with purified RO drinking water.
- **Security**: 24/7 CCTV surveillance, biometric attendance system, full-time female resident wardens, and round-the-clock security personnel.
- **Amenities**: Recreation hall with TV, indoor games, solar water heaters, medical first-aid clinic with visiting lady doctor, and Wi-Fi connectivity.
- **Hostel Timings**: In-time in the evening is strictly 6:30 PM. Outing permissions require parent/guardian approval via the warden.
